TICKET TITLE:
Suspected Phishing Email

CATEGORY:
Security / Email 

PRIORITY:
High

ENVIRONMENT:
Device: Desktop PC
Operating System: Windows 10
Email Platform: Company email system
Location: Office Environment


--------------------------------

USER ISSUE:
User reports receiving a suspicious email that appears to request account credentials and includes a clickable link.

---------------------------------

INITIAL CHECKS:
- Asked the user not to click any links or download attachments.
- Confirmed the email sender address did not match a legitimate company domain.
- Verified the email contained urgent language requesting immediate action.
--------------------------------

TROUBLESHOOTING STEPS:
1. Instructed the user to forward the email to the IT security team for review.
2. Verified the email headers indicated an external sender.
3. Confirmed the user had not interacted with the email.
4. Advised the user to delete the email from their inbox.
5. Educated the user on identifying common phishing indicators.
--------------------------------

RESOLUTION:
The email was identified as a phishing attempt and reported to the IT security team. No user credentials were compromised. No further action required at this time

--------------------------------

OUTCOME:
User confirmed the email was deleted and acknowledged guidance on avoiding phishing attempts in the future.
--------------------------------

TICKET STATUS:
Resolved
